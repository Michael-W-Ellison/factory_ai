Recycling Factory - Audio Assets
=================================

Place audio files in these directories:

ui/
  click.wav       - UI button clicks
  hover.wav       - UI hover sounds
  open.wav        - Menu opening
  close.wav       - Menu closing
  error.wav       - Error feedback
  success.wav     - Success feedback

game/
  build_place.wav     - Building placement
  build_complete.wav  - Construction complete
  demolish.wav        - Building demolition
  research_complete.wav - Research finished
  research_start.wav  - Research started
  money_gain.wav      - Gained money
  money_loss.wav      - Lost money

robot/
  select.wav      - Robot selected
  move.wav        - Robot movement
  collect.wav     - Material collection
  deposit.wav     - Material deposit
  low_battery.wav - Low battery warning

alerts/
  warning.wav     - General warning
  danger.wav      - Danger alert
  inspection.wav  - Inspection starting
  police.wav      - Police alert

ambient/
  factory_hum.wav - Factory ambience
  city.wav        - City ambience

music/
  menu_theme.ogg  - Main menu music
  gameplay.ogg    - Normal gameplay music
  tense.ogg       - High suspicion music
  victory.ogg     - Victory music
  defeat.ogg      - Game over music

Supported formats: .wav, .ogg, .mp3
The game will run without audio files (graceful degradation).
